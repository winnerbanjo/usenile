How to Change Background Image of "Talk to an Expert" section

  1. Go to Themes on the sidebar on the merchant dashboard.
  2. Click on Open Website Builder on the bottom right corner of the Theme page.
  3. Click on Hero under Add Section.
  4. Click on Add Image on the right side.
  5. Select the image you want to add as the background image.
  6. Click on Publish to save.
RESULT

How to Add the Store Logo Image on the Navigation Bar

  1. Click on Store Information on the sidebar.
  2. Click on Edit Store Information.
  3. Click on the image to select the image you want to use as your store logo.
  4. After the image is uploaded, click Save Store Settings.
RESULT

How to Add Your Social Handles

  1. Click on Store Information.
  2. Click on Edit Store Information.
  3. Scroll down to the Social Connections section.
  4. Enter your business social media profile links.
  5. Note Only links added here will appear in the website footer.
  6. Click Save Store Settings.
RESULT

How to Add Pages to Your Store

  1. Click on Pages on the sidebar.
  2. Click Add New Page.
  3. Enter the page name and click Initialize Page Design.
  4. Type the page content and click Publish Changes.
  5. You can create as many pages as needed; they automatically appear in the footer.
RESULT

How to Setup WhatsApp Floating Button

  1. Click Store Information.
  2. Enter a WhatsApp number and save the store settings.
  3. Go to Tools on the sidebar.
  4. Open Storefront Contact and enable the WhatsApp Floating Button.
RESULT

How to Setup Payment Method

  1. Click Payments on the sidebar.
  2. Click Set Payment Method.
  3. Enable card payments, bank transfers, payment on delivery, or any combination of these methods.
